Protein density versus cost for vegetarian protein sources

Scatter plot. Horizontal axis is grams of protein per 100 calories. Vertical axis is cost per gram of protein on a logarithmic scale. Switch to the table view for the same data as text.

Prices are estimates, not observed prices. They are plausible US supermarket figures seeded so the chart works out of the box, dated . Grocery prices vary by region, chain, and week — edit data/prices.js with numbers off your own receipts and this becomes genuinely yours.

Nutrition comes from USDA FoodData Central and manufacturer labels; brand items vary. Scope is lacto-ovo vegetarian. DIAAS scores protein quality — how much of the protein your body can actually use. Below about 0.75 the food is limited by one amino acid, so a high position on the chart overstates it. Wheat protein (seitan, vital wheat gluten) is the sharpest example: superb on both axes, poor quality alone.
